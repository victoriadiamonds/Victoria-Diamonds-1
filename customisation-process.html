Customisation Process

Work one-to-one with your jewellery designer, from the first design concept to the finished piece.

01Design 02Quote 03Deposit payment 04CAD approval 05Production 06Quality check 07Remaining payment 08Delivery
  1. 01 / 08

    Design

    Share your ideas with your designer and explore diamond shapes, metals and settings.

  2. 02 / 08

    Quote

    Review the design concept, stone specifications, sizing and price before you decide to proceed.

  3. 03 / 08

    Deposit payment

    Approve your quote and pay the agreed deposit so we can prepare your technical drawings.

  4. 04 / 08

    CAD approval

    Review your piece from several angles in its computer-aided design (CAD) drawings. Your approval gives the workshop the go-ahead.

  5. 05 / 08

    Production

    Our jewellers craft your approved design, set the stones and finish the metal.

  6. 06 / 08

    Quality check

    We inspect the finished piece, including the setting and finish, against your approved design.

  7. 07 / 08

    Remaining payment

    Once the piece passes inspection, we arrange any outstanding balance before dispatch or collection.

  8. 08 / 08

    Delivery

    We confirm the delivery details with you and arrange insured shipping or collection.
