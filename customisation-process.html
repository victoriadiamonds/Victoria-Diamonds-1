Customisation Process

Work one-to-one with your jewellery designer, from the first design concept to the finished piece.

01Design and quote 02CAD and production 03Quality check and delivery
  1. 01

    Design and quote

    Share your ideas with our design team. Review the initial concept and confirm your main diamond specifications, sizing and quote.

  2. 02

    CAD and production

    After you approve the quote and place your deposit, we prepare a CAD drawing for your approval. Once you approve it, we start crafting your piece.

  3. 03

    Quality check and delivery

    We inspect the finished piece, arrange any remaining payment and confirm dispatch or collection with you.
