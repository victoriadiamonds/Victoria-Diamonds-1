Bespoke Jewellery FAQ

Your commission

How does the 50% deposit work? Is it refundable?

Confirm your centre stone specifications, ring size, initial 2D design and quote in writing. The 50% deposit is non-refundable once paid. We reserve your exact diamond and start CAD modelling that day. The remaining 50% is payable at the arranged in-person handover. Your statutory rights are unaffected.

How do I approve my diamond and design?

Review multiple rounds of CAD drawings and the official IGI report for your reserved diamond. Our jewellers start crafting after your final design approval and verify the stone with you before setting.

Can I add an engraving?

Complimentary inner-band engraving is included. Share your preferred wording once the design is finalised.

Can I choose natural or lab-grown diamonds, or use my own stone?

We work with both natural and lab-grown diamonds. A jeweller can assess an heirloom stone before agreeing to reset it. We confirm timing for each commission with its quote.

Pricing and handover

What does the quote cover? Who covers transit risk?

Your bespoke quote includes operating costs, delivery and full transit insurance up to in-person handover. There are no extra delivery charges on arrival.

Which payment methods do you accept?

Pay by Visa or Mastercard using the secure payment link. Payment settles to Victoria Diamonds’ company account.

Returns and care

Can I return or resize my bespoke ring?

Bespoke rings are made to your agreed design, ring size and diamond specifications and cannot be returned for a change of mind. Our Asia workshop offers repair and resizing for £130, taking 2–3 months. For resizing or minor adjustments, we can recommend reputable local UK jewellers at your own expense. Rights for faulty or misdescribed goods are unaffected.

What does the workmanship warranty cover?

The 12-month workmanship warranty covers manufacturing defects or discrepancies from the approved specification. Normal wear, accidental damage and elective alterations are assessed separately. Statutory rights are unaffected.
